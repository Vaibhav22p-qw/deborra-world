Customer support

Frequently asked questions.

When will DEBORRA launch?

The first assortment and release date will be shared when they are confirmed. Products are planned in phases, beginning with a focused hair-care range.

Can I place an order or preorder?

The collection is in development. Online orders and preorders will open when the first products and fulfilment are ready.

Which products are in development?

Our original concept is a rosemary hair oil. We are also exploring amla oil, oil blends and a hair and scalp serum. Other hair-care and wellness formats are part of the longer-term exploration.

Where can I see ingredients and directions?

Concept pages share the current direction. Final ingredients, usage, directions, warnings and product-specific FAQs will be published alongside each confirmed product.

Where will DEBORRA deliver?

Delivery locations, timelines, charges and tracking information will be published when direct ordering opens.

What will launch emails include?

The launch list is for people who opt in to receive product announcements, educational content, brand updates and occasional offers. Emails will include an unsubscribe option when signups open.

How can I contact DEBORRA?

Call us at +91 77150 38107 for product and launch enquiries.

How do I place an order?

Choose a product and available size in the shop, then open your bag and continue to checkout. Available payment methods are shown there.

Can I use a discount code?

Enter your code in the bag or during checkout. The price summary will show any discount that applies to your order.

How do I track my order?

Open the order status link in your confirmation or shipping message, or sign in to track an order.

Where can I see shipping fees and return terms?

Checkout shows the delivery options for your address. See Shipping & returns for the current policies.

Where are ingredients and directions listed?

Each product page lists the available ingredient information, directions, warnings and product FAQs. Use the final product label for application or serving instructions.

How can I manage DEBORRA emails?

Use the unsubscribe link in any DEBORRA marketing email. Questions can be directed to customer support.

Have another question? Contact DEBORRA
