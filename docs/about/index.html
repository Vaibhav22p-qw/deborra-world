Our story

The making of DEBORRA.

An Indian consumer brand taking shape around thoughtful hair care, wellness and everyday personal care.

Why we started

We wanted everyday care to feel useful, considered and approachable. DEBORRA pairs an emphasis on product quality and clear information with distinctive packaging and a modern experience.

What’s in the name

DEBORRA is our stylised spelling of Deborah, a name commonly associated with the Hebrew word for bee. We take inspiration from its sense of diligence and purposeful creation. DEBORRA World is the umbrella for the ranges we may develop over time.

Where we’re going

We’re beginning with a focused hair-care assortment and will learn from how people use and buy it. Over time, we aim to build a trusted Indian consumer brand across carefully chosen personal-care and wellness formats.

We’re starting online. As the range and our operations mature, we may explore selected retail and salon partnerships and bring DEBORRA to more markets.
