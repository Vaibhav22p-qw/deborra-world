A simpler hair-care routine

A practical way to think about the steps you use and the time you have for them.

Start with the routine you have

Think about the steps you already make time for. Write down when you wash, when you style and whether you use any products between those moments. A short list makes it easier to see where each step fits.

Give each product a purpose

Look for the product’s intended use and directions. Check where it goes, when to apply it and whether the label says to rinse or leave it in. Keep the packaging or a photo of the directions somewhere easy to find.

Observe what works in your day

When you add a product, notice how the application feels and whether the step fits into your routine. Keep track of the details that matter to you, such as texture, scent, time and ease of use.
